# The Peanut Butter Sandwhich

 ## Assumption
 - The Peanut Butter jar, knife, and bread are present.

 ## Steps
 - Open the peanut butter jar
 - Remove the seal
 - Remove the twist tie on the bread
 - open the bag
 - grab the bread
 - Put down the bread
 - pick up the knife
 - cut the bread 2 times  
 - put down the knife
 - Grab the next two pieces of bread down adjecent to each other
 - place the bread down
 - Grab the knife and stick it in the peanut butter jar
 - scoop peanut butter with knife
 - spread peanut butter on left slice
 - put the knife to the side of the bread
 - place slice 2 on slice 1

 ## Done When
 -Two slices of bread are on one another with peanut butter inbetween
